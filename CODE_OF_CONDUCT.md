# Code of conduct

Hearthscan is a small project and wants to stay a friendly place, for people who know
networks inside out and for people asking their first question.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain instead of correcting.
- Keep it about the work. Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwanted personal attention, here or in
  private messages about the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's network details, addresses or names.
- Hearthscan is for checking your own network. Asking for help attacking networks or
  devices you do not own is not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, use the
contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
